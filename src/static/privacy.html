Privacy

This page describes exactly what the site does today - nothing more.

  • No user accounts, and no sign-up of any kind.
  • When you save a build, we store only the part ids you chose, an optional build name and optional notes, under a random share token. There is nothing to tie a saved build back to you.
  • The site does not use browser storage (no cookies, no localStorage, no sessionStorage) to identify or track you.
  • Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives the visitor's IP address when a page loads.
  • Product images are proxied through our own server, not loaded directly from retailer sites.
  • Standard server logs (the kind any web server keeps) are all we retain about requests.
  • No analytics and no ad trackers of any kind.
  • No price alerts and no email sign-ups - we don't collect an email address to notify you of anything.
