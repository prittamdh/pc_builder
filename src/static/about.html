About & disclaimer

PC Builder 2 compares PC part prices across Indian retailers and checks whether the parts you pick will actually fit and work together.

Where the prices come from

Prices are collected automatically from each retailer's public pages, and may lag behind what the shop is currently showing. We are not affiliated with any retailer - every listing links to the shop's own product page, and we earn nothing from your purchase.

Always verify the price, stock and specs with the retailer before buying. Treat everything here as a starting point for comparison, not a live checkout.

Compatibility checks

When a part's spec sheet doesn't publish a value we need (say, a case's maximum GPU length), the checker marks that item "unverified" instead of silently passing it. An unverified item is not a pass - it means we couldn't check it, so double check that spec yourself.
